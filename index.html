Frequently asked questions

Study Abroad FAQs

Which countries does Orient Consultancy support?

Orient Consultancy provides guidance for South Korea, Japan, the United Kingdom, Australia, Canada, Germany, New Zealand, Malta, Cyprus and other European study destinations.

Do you provide IELTS and PTE preparation guidance?

Yes. We help students plan for IELTS and PTE preparation and understand the score requirements for their selected institutions and programs.

Can you help me choose the right university and course?

Yes. Our counseling process includes profile assessment, career counseling and university matching based on academic background, budget, career goals and destination preferences.

Do you support visa documentation and applications?

Yes. We provide documentation checklists, application support, visa guidance, interview preparation where relevant and pre-departure assistance.

How do I start my study abroad application?

Start with a counseling session. We will review your profile, shortlist suitable options and provide a step-by-step application roadmap.
