Orient Consultancy Pvt. Ltd. is a trusted educational consultancy established since 2014 with the mission of guiding Nepalese students toward global academic opportunities. Located conveniently beside PK Campus in Bagbazar, Kathmandu, we specialize in student visa processing for leading study destinations including South Korea, Australia, Japan, the USA, Germany, and other European countries.

Our key strength lies in our strong network in South Korea, allowing us to offer guaranteed visa success for students aspiring to study there. With a year of successful operations, we have quickly become a preferred choice for students seeking reliable guidance and transparent services.
